## What this task is

The reader's backend answers what the frontend asks to see:

- Each `open` (shelf, book, song, setlist, settings) is answered with its view
  (`103 shelf`, `104 book`, `105 sheet`, `106 setlist`, `107 settings`). The
  answer is read from the last sync's snapshot, the engine (the web app's
  music, in goja) and the store (the reader's own state).
- The person's choices at the stand are kept in the store and answered with
  what they redraw:
  - a key for the song;
  - the place in a setlist;
  - a voicing hidden or shown again;
  - *Show all again*;
  - shapes shown or hidden.
- `signOut` and `pair` stay `reader.pairing`'s.

## Why it needs to be done

Until now the backend answers `hello` with `state` and nothing else
(app_folder Decision 3). The QML screens (`reader.qml_chart`,
`reader.song_screen`, `reader.browse_screens`, `reader.play`) are tested
against scripted messages. On a device they draw only what this task sends.
It is the join between `reader.sync` (what came down), `reader.engine` (the
music) and `reader.store` (what the person chose). Without it, the M7 promise
— "reads … any song at any key with the shapes of the instrument in use" —
has nothing behind it.

## Constraints / requirements

1. **The music comes from the bundle, never from Go.** Keys, offsets, key
   names, shape resolution, frets and diagram models all come from the
   engine. The Go side keeps no music rule (design §4, "No port of the music
   core").
   - It may read titles, artists and setlist items with `cifra.md/go`, which
     is the format, not the app's music. This is the design delta.
   - The bundle's changes are additive and stay at `hostApi` 1, because no
     bundle has been published (`reader.payload` has not landed). Decision 4
     gives the details.
   - In `entry.js`:
     - `sheet` `opts` gains `variations` (the `songs.json` per-tuning map,
       passed as `reading.variations`). When given, it wins over `variation`.
     - `sheet` `opts` gains `savedOffset` (an integer or null).
     - The result gains `tuningId` (the session's).
     - The result's `key` gains `saved`: the key named as the song would be
       read at `savedOffset`, by the same rule as `named`. It is null when no
       `savedOffset` is given.
     - Each resolved shape gains `frets` (`shorthandOf` of its fingering's
       frets).
     - `setlist`'s result gains `rows`: every item of `setlistRows` in order,
       as `{ at, number, title, path, key, note, missing }`.
2. **A song's offset, in order of precedence:**
   1. the `open`'s `offset` when given (for this answer only, never kept);
   2. when played (`open.play`), the item's `key` as `TargetKey` (workflow
      12 §The key — a keyless song carries it as `keyNote`, read at the
      offsets below);
   3. `store.Key(bookId, path)`;
   4. `songs.json`'s `Transposition` for the ref `<bookId>/<path>`;
   5. otherwise 0.

   `SavedOffset` is that transposition whenever `songs.json` has the song.
   `Variations` is its `Variation` map, and `Preferences` is
   `Snapshot.Preferences()`.
3. **What each `open` answers.** The view's snapshot is taken under a hold:
   `Hold(bookId)`, then `Snapshot()`, so a pass between them can only remove
   the book from the snapshot, never from under it.
   - `shelf`: a `ShelfBook` for each book on `Snapshot.Shelf()` that has a
     clone (`Book(id)` non-nil), in shelf order.
     - `title` is the `Label`, or `""` (the frontend words *Untitled book*).
     - `songs` and `setlists` are the counts of `Paths()` ending
       `.cifra.md` and `.setlist.md`. No file is parsed.
   - `book`:
     - setlists by path, each with its title and count (song and unlinked
       items, from `cifra.md/go` `ParseSetlist`);
     - then songs by path, code point by code point, each with its title and
       its `artist` property (`cifra.md/go` `Parse`; `""` when absent or
       unreadable).
     - The listing is kept in memory per (bookId, head), so a second
       opening parses nothing.
   - `song`:
     - the engine's `Sheet`, mapped onto §6:
       - each shape's `key` becomes `chord`, and `diagram` becomes `model`;
       - `frets` comes from the bundle;
       - `hidden` is `store.Hidden({tuningId, chord, frets})`;
       - a pending chord keeps its place with `model`/`frets` null and
         `hidden` false;
       - `unvoiced` chords are not shapes.
     - `title`/`artist` are `""` when null.
     - With `play`:
       - the setlist is read from the same snapshot;
       - `play` is `{ setlist, at, of: <playable count>, next }`;
       - `next` is the next playable item's `{ title, key, note }`, or null
         at the last.
   - `setlist`: the engine's `Setlist(text, path, Paths())` `rows`, mapped
     to items. `at` is `store.Place(bookId, path)` when kept.
   - `settings`: `{ account: Session().Account, version: Deps.Version,
     hiddenShapes: HiddenCount() }`.
4. **The view keeps its version, and its book is held, until an `open`
   leaves it.**
   - The views hold one current context: the book id, the snapshot it was
     read from, the release, and the open song (path, offset source, play)
     when there is one.
   - A `key` or a shape message redraws the open song from that same
     snapshot. A sync never changes the song on screen (§5.3; workflow 19
     rule 5).
   - **Play** keeps the snapshot of the first played `open` for that
     (book, setlist) until an `open` without `play` for it. Every song of
     the setlist is then read from the version Play started with.
   - An `open` of a book the latest snapshot lacks, while the context holds
     that book, is read from the held snapshot (workflow 19 §When something
     has changed).
   - An `open` of another book, of the shelf or of settings releases the
     hold, exactly once.
5. **The other messages.**
   - `key{offset}`: `SetKey(bookId, path, offset)` for the open song. It
     answers the redrawn sheet; `null` reads at the saved transposition
     again. The open's one-off `offset` and a play target are dropped for
     that redraw (Decision 6).
   - `hideShape`/`showShape`: `store.Hide`/`Show` of the triple as sent.
   - `showAllShapes`: `ShowAll`.
   - After `hideShape`, `showShape` or `showAllShapes`: the redrawn `sheet`
     when a song is the current view, else `settings`.
   - `shapesShown{shown}`: `SetShapesShown`, answered with `state` (which
     carries `shapesShown`; the sheet does not change).
   - `place`: `SetPlace`, answered with nothing.
   - Any of these with no song open, where one is needed, changes nothing and
     answers nothing.
   - A store write that fails answers nothing and leaves the connection up.
6. **Offline at start, shapes still drawn.** `sync.(*Syncer).Run` calls
   `loadInstrument` over the home book on disk before its first pass. A
   reader started without Wi-Fi then reads with the instrument it synced
   last; until now the instrument was loaded only after `getHomeBook`
   succeeded. With nothing on disk it loads nothing, as today.
7. **No engine, no music.** `Deps.Engine` is an interface (`Sheet`,
   `Setlist`) that `*engine.Engine` satisfies, and it may be nil (no bundle
   installed, or it failed to open: `main.go` L364–370).
   - With no engine, `shelf`, `book` and `settings` answer as usual.
   - `song` and `setlist` answer the nearest view (Constraint 8).
   - `main.go` passes nil, never a typed-nil pointer.
8. **An `open` that cannot be answered as asked** answers the nearest view
   that can be: song or setlist → its book → the shelf. This covers the book
   gone and not held, the file absent (`object.ErrFileNotFound`), the song
   `ErrUnreadable`, no engine, and an engine error. No new message type is
   added (Decision 7).
9. **No song text in logs or errors.** The views log nothing (protocol L199–
   201; store L125–127). Paths travel only in the payloads to the frontend.
10. **Sequential and bounded.** The views run on Serve's loop goroutine, one
    message at a time. Each engine call gets a context derived from Serve's,
    so terminate cancels a call in flight.
11. **The reader writes nothing to the account.** Nothing here pushes or
    writes the home book (design §5.4).

sync). The QML screens are tested by their own tasks.

Deferred work: none. Misses found in the background are `reader.missed_shapes`.
`signOut`/`pair` are `reader.pairing`. Drawing all of this is the QML tasks'
job.

## Decisions

1. **Titles and artists for the book listing come from `cifra.md/go`, not the
   bundle.**
   - The bundle has no header-only call. `sheet` parses the whole song (~80 ms
     on an rM2, design §4.2), so a book of 300 songs would take ~24 s to open,
     against workflow 19's "open a book — 1" redraw.
   - `cifra.md/go` is the format's own Go implementation. The service already
     reads songs with it, and it is held to the corpus. A title and the
     `artist` property are format data, not the app's music.
   - The listing is memoised per (book, head).
   - Rejected: a bundle `meta(text)` call. It is still a goja parse per song.
   - Rejected: caching the bundle's titles on disk. The first opening would
     still be slow, and there would be a cache to invalidate.
   - Rejected: a hand-written header scanner in Go. It is a second, unchecked
     reading of the format.
2. **The bundle grows, the Go side stays music-free.**
   - The sheet needs frets (the name of a hidden voicing), the tuning id, the
     saved key's name, and the variation for the instrument's tuning.
   - Computing any of them in Go would port the music core (`shorthandOf`,
     `transposedKeyName`, the variation's tuning).
   - So the bundle returns them, and `variations` passes the whole
     `songs.json` map, so the bundle chooses by its own session's tuning
     atomically.
   - Rejected: deriving frets from the diagram model in Go.
   - Rejected: keeping the `Load`'s tuning id in Go. A pass may `Load`
     between the choice and the sheet.
3. **`106` items lose `offset`.**
   - It is the offset Play would read each item at. Computing it means a goja
     parse of every song of the setlist when the setlist opens (a second on
     an rM2 for twelve songs).
   - Nothing on the setlist screen draws it. Play's sheet carries its own
     offset.
   - Each item's `key` is the item's own property, as the web app's
     `setlistRows` gives it.
   - Rejected: keeping the field and computing it lazily. That adds a field
     no screen reads.
4. **`hostApi` stays 1.** Every change is additive, and the host and the bundle
   land in one commit. No bundle has been published to a device
   (`reader.payload`, `reader.self_update` pending). A bump would only make
   the engine refuse its own build. Once self-update ships, an incompatible
   change bumps it (design §8).
5. **The view keeps its snapshot; a new `open` takes the latest, except in Play
   or for a book only the held snapshot has.**
   - This is the reading of workflow 19 §When something has changed: a song
     shows its new version on its next opening, Play keeps its version until
     Back, and a gone book's song stays readable until Back.
   - The views' context is the "screen" that `sync.Hold` was built for.
   - Rejected: always the latest snapshot. It would break Play's version and
     the gone book's song.
   - Rejected: always the held snapshot within a book. A re-opened song would
     never show its new version.
6. **A `key` during Play is kept for the song, but Play still opens at the
   setlist's key.**
   - The person chose a key for this song (design §5.4), so it is kept.
   - The setlist's key is what Play shows on the key button (workflow 19
     L298–299), so the next Play of the song opens at it again.
7. **An unanswerable `open` answers the nearest view, not an error message.**
   - The cases are rare (a race with a pass, a non-UTF-8 file, no bundle).
   - §6 has no error payload for views, and the frontend draws whatever view
     it receives.
   - Rejected: a `sheet` with an `error` word. Every QML screen would then
     need an error state, for cases the listing almost never offers.
8. **`shapesShown` answers `state`, and `place` answers nothing.**
   - `state` is where §6 carries `shapesShown`. The sheet carries every shape
     either way (protocol Decision 2), so a re-drawn sheet would be the same
     bytes.
   - `place` only records where Back returns. The frontend is already
     showing the song.
9. **The instrument is loaded from disk at `Run`'s start (Constraint 6).**
   - Offline first: a reader started without Wi-Fi would otherwise read every
     song with no shapes until a pass succeeds.
   - It reuses sync's `loadInstrument` and its digest skip, so the first
     online pass loads nothing twice.
   - Rejected: the views loading the instrument themselves. That would put a
     second owner on `Load`.
10. **A shelf book without a clone is not listed.** It cannot be opened yet;
    there is "nothing to tap that leads nowhere" (workflow 19 §A book's rule
    for letters, applied to rows). It appears on the shelf's next opening
    after the clone lands (sync Decision 4).
