Workflow 10

Share

A book on Estante starts private, and nobody opens it without an account. Its owner invites people by link, as readers or editors, up to fifty per book; the owner sees who joined through each invitation, and can remove anyone alone. A made link goes out with Send where the device can share, or Copy; a book nobody has joined yet leads with the invitation.
