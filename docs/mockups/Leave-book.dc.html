Leaving a book

A reader or an editor ends their own membership with Leave this book, below Remove from my shelf, or with Leave on a Mine on Estante row; an owner is never offered it. The confirmation counts what would be lost and offers a fork or the download first; the membership ends on Estante before the copy leaves this device, and it needs a connection.
