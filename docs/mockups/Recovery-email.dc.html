The recovery email

An account starts with a passkey and no email. Its owner adds the address the sign-in link goes to from the account screen; it is written only when the emailed verification link is opened, which confirms and does not sign in. Without one, the last passkey cannot be removed, and deleting the account is confirmed with a passkey instead of a typed name.
