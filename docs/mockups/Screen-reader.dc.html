How a screen reader reads a chart

The order a chart is read aloud is decided, not left to the markup. A sung line interleaves chord then the words it sits over; a chart line is its measures in order; a diagram is its fingering and whose shape it is; a conflict is bounded by a word. Each device draws a real reading-view chart beside the exact text a screen reader announces — a chord by name, padding and the centred hyphen silent, the bar glyph and the git markers never spoken.
