Workflow 12

Setlists

A setlist is a Markdown file in a book: a numbered list of relative links to songs in that same book, each with an optional key and note. Play goes through it one song at a time, full screen, in the setlist's key, skipping any song that has gone missing; pressing a song in the list starts Play at that song. Shared, it is the book's invitation that opens on the setlist. A setlist is made from the Setlists tab, or from a song with Add to setlist, which then says the song is in it.
