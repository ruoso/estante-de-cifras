Workflow 17

Leaving

Take your data out — every book downloaded as the git repository it already is, offline and as often as you like — then delete the account for good. Deleting removes the sessions and passkeys, deletes the books you own for everyone, ends your access to books others own, and is the one action that waits for a connection. Guarded by typing the account's name.
