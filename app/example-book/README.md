# Example Book

These songs were written for Estante, to show what the app does before you
add any music of your own. The words and the arrangements are original —
nothing in this book belongs to anyone else.

Each song is voiced for guitar, cavaquinho and ukulele, so you can switch
instruments and still find shapes to play. Between them the songs show the
things a cifra.md book holds: sections, words under chords, more than one
voicing of the same song, and a setlist you can play straight through.

Open the example book to put it on your shelf. It is a plain local book, and
you can remove it whenever you like.
